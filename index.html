👑 Ludo Sultan: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the Ludo Sultan app ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and your choices. The app is published on Google Play by Khawar Hunny Hameed (individual developer).

1. Information we collect

  • Random device/player ID. On first launch the app creates a random identifier stored on your device, plus a short public "friend code" derived from it. It is used to recognize you on our game server (online matches, friends, messages). It is not your phone's IMEI, advertising ID, phone number or Google account.
  • Profile information you choose. Display name, chosen avatar, level, wins, games played and selected avatar frame. These are shown to other players.
  • Game progress. Coins, diamonds, items, settings and statistics are stored locally on your device.
  • Social data (online features only). Friend list, friend requests, blocked players, recent opponents, club membership, private messages (DMs) between friends, chat-room messages and gifts. DMs and social lists are stored on our server so they are available when you reconnect. Room and match chat is relayed in real time and only the last few messages of a room are kept in server memory.
  • Reports. If you report a player, we store the report (reporter ID, reported ID, reason and short context) to review abuse.
  • Microphone / voice chat. Voice chat is optional and off by default. The microphone is only used after you tap the mic button and grant Android's microphone permission. Voice audio is sent directly between players' devices (peer-to-peer, WebRTC) and, where a direct connection is not possible, through a relay (TURN) server that only forwards encrypted audio. We do not record or store voice audio.
  • Technical data. Like any internet service, our server receives your IP address while you are connected; it is used only to deliver the connection and is not stored in our database.

2. How we use information

  • To run online matches, chat rooms, voice chat, friends, messages and gifts.
  • To keep the community safe (profanity filter, block, mute, report handling).
  • To fix bugs and keep the service running.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The app contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files.
  • In-app purchases in this version are test/simulated only; no payment information is collected by the app. If real purchases are added, payments will be processed by Google Play, and we will never see your card details.

4. Sharing

Your display name, avatar, level, statistics, friend code, online status and the messages you send are visible to the other players you interact with. Otherwise, we only disclose data if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or use "Reset data". Server-side social data (profile, friends, DMs, club, reports) is kept while your account is active. To request deletion of your server data, email us with your friend code (shown in the Friends screen); we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The app includes open chat and voice chat with other players and is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Connections to our server should use encrypted WebSocket (WSS) in production, and voice audio is encrypted end-to-end by WebRTC (DTLS-SRTP). No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

8. Your choices

  • You can play offline (vs Computer / Local) without any online data being sent.
  • You can keep the microphone off, mute or block any player, and revoke the microphone permission in Android Settings at any time.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change and significant changes will be announced in the app.
