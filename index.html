👑 Ludo Sultan: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information the Ludo Sultan app ("the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and your choices. The app is published on Google Play by Khawar Hunny Hameed (individual developer).

1. Information we collect

  • Random device/player ID. On first launch the app creates a random identifier stored on your device, plus a short public "friend code" derived from it. It is used to recognize you on our game server (online matches, friends, messages). It is not your phone's IMEI, advertising ID, phone number or Google account.
  • Profile information you choose. Display name, chosen avatar, level, wins, games played and selected avatar frame. These are shown to other players.
  • Game progress. Coins, diamonds, items, settings and statistics are stored locally on your device. If you create an account, a copy of this progress (cloud save) is also stored on our server so you can restore it on another device.
  • Account (optional). Playing as a guest needs no account. If you sign up with email and password, we store your email address, display name and a salted scrypt hash of your password (never the password itself), plus hashed sign-in session tokens. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive from that provider your account ID, email address, name and profile picture URL; we verify the sign-in token with the provider and do not receive your Google or Facebook password.
  • Event scores. Points and wins in seasonal events (for example Halloween or the Festival of Lights) and online tournament results, used for leaderboards and prizes.
  • Social data (online features only). Friend list, friend requests, blocked players, recent opponents, club membership, private messages (DMs) between friends, chat-room messages and gifts. DMs and social lists are stored on our server so they are available when you reconnect. Room and match chat is relayed in real time and only the last few messages of a room are kept in server memory.
  • Reports. If you report a player, we store the report (reporter ID, reported ID, reason and short context) to review abuse.
  • Microphone / voice chat. Voice chat is optional and off by default. The microphone is only used after you tap the mic button and grant Android's microphone permission. Voice audio is sent directly between players' devices (peer-to-peer, WebRTC) and, where a direct connection is not possible, through a relay (TURN) server that only forwards encrypted audio. We do not record or store voice audio.
  • Technical data. Like any internet service, our server receives your IP address while you are connected; it is used only to deliver the connection and is not stored in our database.

2. How we use information

  • To run online matches, chat rooms, voice chat, friends, messages and gifts.
  • To keep the community safe (profanity filter, block, mute, report handling).
  • To fix bugs and keep the service running.

3. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The app contains no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. The only ads are optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, shown only when you choose to watch one for a bonus; AdMob may process device and advertising identifiers under Google's policies.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files.
  • In-app purchases in this version are test/simulated only; no payment information is collected by the app. If real purchases are added, payments will be processed by Google Play, and we will never see your card details.

4. Sharing

Your display name, avatar, level, statistics, friend code, online status and the messages you send are visible to the other players you interact with. Otherwise, we only disclose data if required by law.

5. Data retention and deletion

Local game data stays on your device until you uninstall the app or use "Reset data". Server-side data (account, cloud save, profile, friends, DMs, club, event scores, reports) is kept while your account or player ID is active.

5a. How to delete your account and data

In the app (instant): tap 👤 Account (bottom bar of the home screen) → Delete account. Email accounts must confirm with their password. This immediately and permanently deletes from our server:

  • your account (email, name, password hash, linked Google/Facebook ID) and all sign-in sessions;
  • your cloud save;
  • your public profile, friend list and friend requests, recent players, club membership, private messages and event leaderboard scores.

You can also choose to wipe the game progress stored on your phone at the same time. Reports you filed against others may be kept up to 90 days for safety review, without your account details.

Without the app: email Khawarhunny26@gmail.com with the subject "Ludo Sultan data deletion" and include your account email or your friend code (shown in the Friends screen). We will confirm and delete your data within 30 days.

Google / Facebook sign-in: deleting your Ludo Sultan account removes the link on our side. You can also remove Ludo Sultan's access in your Google Account (Security → Third-party connections) or in Facebook (Settings → Apps and Websites). Facebook data deletion requests follow the same steps above.

Server backups, if any, are overwritten within 30 days.

6. Children

The app includes open chat and voice chat with other players and is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

Connections to our server use encrypted WebSocket (WSS); passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes and session tokens only as SHA-256 hashes, and voice audio is encrypted end-to-end by WebRTC (DTLS-SRTP). No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

8. Your choices

  • You can play offline (vs Computer / Local) without any online data being sent, and you can play online as a guest without creating an account.
  • You can keep the microphone off, mute or block any player, and revoke the microphone permission in Android Settings at any time.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change and significant changes will be announced in the app.
